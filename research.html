Research & Publications

My research focuses on environmental economics, health impacts of pollution, groundwater contamination, ecosystem valuation, behavioural dimensions of environmental decision-making, and applied econometrics. My work integrates interdisciplinary methods, large-scale datasets, and quantitative modelling approaches to address sustainability and development challenges.

Research Areas

Environmental Economics

Groundwater contamination, pollution impacts, and sustainability analysis.

Health Economics

Economic burden and health impacts of environmental pollution.

Applied Econometrics

Bayesian methods, SEM, SUBP models, and impact evaluation.

Development Economics

Livelihoods, education, poverty, and rural development.
